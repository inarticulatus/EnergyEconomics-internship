# Energy Economics Research Internship
Overview

This repository encompasses all reports, datasets, and scripts developed during a research internship focusing on the Indian energy economics landscape. The primary objective was to analyze the interplay between energy consumption patterns, renewable energy dependency, and macroeconomic indicators, especially in the context of the COVID-19 pandemic.
Repository Structure

    data/: Contains raw and processed datasets, including electricity consumption figures, GDP statistics, COVID-19 case numbers, and renewable energy metrics.

    literature_review/: Summaries and analyses of existing research papers and articles relevant to Indian energy economics.

    statistical_analysis/: Jupyter notebooks and scripts performing statistical analyses, visualizations, and modeling.

    tools_scripts/: Utility scripts for data cleaning, transformation, and other preprocessing tasks.

    draft/: Preliminary drafts of reports and findings compiled during the internship.

Key Objectives

    Analyze historical electricity consumption data to identify trends and anomalies.

    Assess the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on energy demand and supply.

    Evaluate the growth and challenges of renewable energy adoption in India.

    Correlate energy consumption patterns with GDP and other economic indicators over the past decade.

Methodology

    Data Collection: Aggregated data from government portals, international energy databases, and COVID-19 tracking sources.

    Data Cleaning: Employed Python scripts to handle missing values, outliers, and ensure consistency across datasets.

    Statistical Analysis: Utilized Jupyter notebooks to perform time-series analyses, regression models, and correlation studies.

    Visualization: Generated plots and charts to depict trends, comparisons, and key insights.

Findings

    A noticeable dip in electricity consumption during the peak months of the COVID-19 lockdown, followed by a gradual recovery.

    Renewable energy sources, particularly solar and wind, showed resilience and continued growth despite the pandemic-induced challenges.

    A strong correlation between GDP growth rates and energy consumption patterns, emphasizing the intertwined nature of economic activity and energy demand.

Future Work

    Incorporate more granular data, such as state-wise energy consumption and sector-specific analyses.

    Explore machine learning models to forecast energy demand under various economic scenarios.

    Assess the long-term implications of policy changes on renewable energy adoption and energy security.
